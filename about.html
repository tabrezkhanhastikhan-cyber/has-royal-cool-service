Our Story

Why We Started

In coastal Vasai-Virar, appliances work harder than almost anywhere in Mumbai. Salt in the air eats copper coils, humidity damages boards, and every summer ACs and fridges run non-stop. When one fails, you need someone who answers the phone, turns up, and fixes the real problem.

That is why H A S Royal Cool Service exists. We started as a small AC and refrigeration repair team and grew by doing one thing well: showing up and getting the job done honestly. Today we repair AC, fridge, washing machine, geyser, microwave, deep freezer, RO, kitchen chimney and LED TV, and install ACs in bulk for offices and shops.

Our promiseExplain first. Quote first. Repair properly. Leave it clean.
