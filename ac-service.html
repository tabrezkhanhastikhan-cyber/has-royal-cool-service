AC Service at your doorstep

A regular AC service is the cheapest way to keep your air conditioner cooling properly and your electricity bill under control. Dust on indoor and outdoor coils, a clogged filter or a blocked drain pipe forces the compressor to work harder, which means weaker cooling and a shorter life for the machine.

Our AC service covers the indoor unit, filters, blower, drain line, outdoor condenser and a gas pressure check. In coastal areas such as Vasai West, Virar West and Nalasopara West we also pay extra attention to salt and humidity damage on outdoor units and copper piping.

What our AC technicians handle

  • AC General & Jet Servicing: High-pressure deep cleaning of indoor & outdoor coils.
  • Gas Charging & Leakage Check: Permanent solution for low cooling with R32/R410A gas.
  • Water Leakage & Noise Fix: Choked drain pipe cleaning & blower balancing.
  • Installation & Shifting: Safe mounting, copper piping & wiring check.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I get my AC serviced?

For regular use, a jet service once before summer and another mid-season is a good rule. If your AC runs most of the day or you live close to the sea, service it every 4 to 6 months.

Do you service all AC brands?

Yes. We service all brand split and window ACs, including inverter models.

Will the technician come to my home?

Yes. All service is done at your doorstep in Vasai, Virar, Nalasopara and Naigaon, East and West.
